# LEVEL 6 – Sales Performance Analytics
### Strong Intermediate

**Company:** NovaMart Electronics  
**Focus:** Ranking, Customer Segmentation, Moving Averages, Better DAX patterns

---

## Learning Objectives
- Write more useful analytical DAX (RANKX, TOPN, moving averages)
- Customer segmentation logic
- ABC analysis / Pareto thinking
- Clean multi-page report with good UX
- Use of variables in DAX

---

## Data Files
- `FactSales.csv` (8,000 rows)
- `DimDate.csv`, `DimStore.csv` (15), `DimProduct.csv` (100), `DimCustomer.csv` (500)

---

## Required Measures (minimum 18)

**Core**
- Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin %, Total Quantity, Total Orders, AOV

**Time Intelligence**
- Sales YTD, Sales PY, YoY Growth %, Sales Rolling 3 Months, Sales Rolling 12 Months

**Ranking & Analytical**
- Product Rank by Sales
- Store Rank by Profit
- Customer Rank by Sales
- Top 10 Products Sales (using TOPN or visual level)

**Customer Analytics**
- New vs Returning Customers (simple version)
- Average Customer Value
- Sales by Loyalty Tier

**Advanced pattern**
- % of Total Sales (using ALL or ALLSELECTED)
- Running Total Sales

---

## Report Requirements (3–4 pages)

1. **Executive Overview** – KPIs + trend + YoY + Region performance
2. **Product Performance** – Ranking, Category contribution, Pareto feel
3. **Customer Insights** – Loyalty, Age Group, New vs Returning, Top Customers
4. **Store Scorecard** – Ranked table with Sales, Profit, Margin, Rank

---

## Business Questions
1. Which products are in the top 10% by sales contribution?
2. Which stores are consistently in the bottom 5 by profit?
3. What is the Rolling 12-month sales trend?
4. How does average order value differ by Loyalty Tier?
5. Which region has the best profit margin?
6. Create a simple ABC classification of products (A = top 20% revenue).

**Estimated Time:** 10–14 hours
